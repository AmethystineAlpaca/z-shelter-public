Z Shelter — READ THIS FIRST: How to open on macOS
================================================

No Godot installation, Terminal commands, or development tools are needed.

1. Extract the entire ZIP. Move Z Shelter.app to Applications or another
   folder you own, then double-click the app.
2. This public prototype build is ad-hoc signed but NOT Apple-notarized.
   On first launch, macOS may say that Apple cannot verify whether
   “Z Shelter” contains malware, with “Done” and “Move to Trash” buttons.
3. If you downloaded this build from our official GitHub release and trust
   its source, click “Done”, not “Move to Trash”.
4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the notice
   that Z Shelter was blocked, and click “Open Anyway”.
5. Authenticate if prompted, then click “Open” in the confirmation dialog.
   macOS normally remembers this exception for this copy of the app.

If “Open Anyway” is missing, try opening the app once more, then return to
Privacy & Security. A managed Mac may require help from its administrator.
Do not disable Gatekeeper globally or run commands to turn off Mac security.
This procedure is for the unverified-app warning above. If macOS says the
app is damaged or will harm your computer, stop and report the exact message.

Once the title screen opens, choose “Begin a new journal”. Use English / 中文
at the top right to change language; the preference is saved. The optional
tutorial explains preparation shopping and the first survival days.

Currently supplied for macOS, with Apple Silicon and Intel executables.
This is a free public prototype. Game source remains private.
